Comprehensive care,

Every service is delivered by the same small, consistent team — people who know your loved one by name, not by room number.

What we provide

Care that covers every need — at home

We provide a full spectrum of daily care within one intimate setting. Unlike large facilities, we don't require residents to navigate between departments. Everything happens here, by people they know and trust.

Memory & cognitive support

Dementia & Memory Care

Our intimate environment is uniquely suited to memory care. With only four residents, our caregivers maintain consistent routines, familiar faces, and a calm atmosphere — all clinically shown to reduce distress in residents with dementia and Alzheimer's.

We are trained in person-centered dementia care approaches and work closely with families and physicians to adapt care as needs evolve.

  • Consistent staff — no rotation of unfamiliar faces
  • Structured daily routines to reduce anxiety
  • Safe, secure living environment
  • Regular family communication and care conferences
  • Coordination with geriatricians and specialists

Personal care

Daily Living Assistance

We provide dignified, respectful assistance with all activities of daily living — from bathing and dressing to grooming and mobility support. Our approach always prioritizes the resident's independence: we help with what is needed, and encourage what is possible.

  • Bathing, dressing, and grooming assistance
  • Mobility and transfer support
  • Incontinence care, handled with full dignity
  • Assistance with ambulation and exercise
  • Personal hygiene and oral care

Health management

Medication Management

Accurate, on-time medication administration is a cornerstone of our care. We maintain detailed medication logs, coordinate refills, and work directly with pharmacies and prescribing physicians to ensure every resident receives exactly what they need, when they need it.

  • Scheduled medication administration with documentation
  • Coordination with prescribing physicians and pharmacists
  • PRN (as-needed) medication monitoring
  • Medication storage and security
  • Side effect monitoring and reporting

Dining & nutrition

Nutritious Meals & Dining

Three home-cooked meals daily, plus snacks — prepared in our kitchen, served at our dining table. We accommodate dietary restrictions, cultural preferences, and personal favorites. Because one of the greatest joys of a good day is a good meal.

  • Three fresh-cooked meals and snacks daily
  • Dietary accommodations: diabetic, low-sodium, pureed, etc.
  • Cultural and personal food preferences honored
  • Dining as a social experience at a shared table
  • Hydration monitoring and encouragement
