Who we serve

Is Lake Stevens AFH the right fit for your family?

We serve seniors and families who want genuine, personalized care in a real home — not a large institution with rotating staff and numbered beds.

Seniors needing daily support

Your parent needs help with bathing, dressing, meals, and mobility — but still deserves independence and dignity in a home that truly feels like theirs.

Loved ones with memory challenges

Dementia and Alzheimer's require calm, consistent environments. Our small home — just 4 residents — means familiar faces, steady routines, and a place that doesn't overwhelm.

Families seeking real peace of mind

You want to know your loved one is safe and genuinely happy — and you want to be able to call any time and speak with someone who actually knows them by name.

Not sure if we're the right fit? We're happy to talk through your loved one's specific needs — no pressure, no sales pitch. Call us at (206) 383-3109 or send us a message.
